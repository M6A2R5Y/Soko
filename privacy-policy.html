Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 2026

1. Overview and Data Controller

Soko Edit operates the online storefront at this domain and provides retail delivery services throughout Kenya, with administrative operations based in Mombasa, Kenya. We respect your personal privacy and comply strictly with the provisions of the Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019.

This Privacy Policy explains what personal data we collect when you browse our catalog, place items in your bag, or submit an order via our website and WhatsApp communication channels.

2. Personal Data We Collect

We collect only the information strictly necessary to process, fulfill, and deliver your orders:

  • Contact Identification: Full name and primary phone number (used for order verification, delivery coordination, and M-Pesa transaction confirmation).
  • Delivery Information: Physical delivery address, county, town, landmark, and specific dispatch instructions.
  • Order Details: Items purchased, quantities, item notes, and total purchase value in Kenyan Shillings (KES).
  • Communications: Order confirmations, inquiries, and customer service correspondence conducted via WhatsApp or phone call.
  • Local Browser Storage: We use your browser local storage solely to retain your shopping bag selections between visits. We do not use third-party tracking cookies or behavioral tracking pixels.

3. How We Collect Your Information

Your information is collected directly from you when you:

  • Add products to your shopping bag on our website.
  • Enter your name, phone number, and delivery location in the checkout form.
  • Transmit your order to our customer support desk via WhatsApp or direct phone call.

4. Purpose and Legal Basis for Processing

We process your personal information under the following legal bases recognized by the Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019:

  • Contractual Performance: Processing your order, verifying stock, arranging dispatch, and fulfilling doorstep delivery.
  • Customer Support: Assisting you with delivery inquiries, tracking numbers, or processing requests under our 7-day return policy.
  • Legal Compliance: Maintaining accurate financial and transactional records as required under Kenyan commercial and tax regulations.

5. Sharing of Information

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data. We disclose your information only to vetted third-party service providers essential for completing your purchase:

  • Courier and Delivery Partners: Designated local logistics services and riders who require your recipient name, phone number, and delivery location to hand over your parcel.
  • Payment Processors and Mobile Networks: Safaricom (M-Pesa), Airtel Money, or licensed Kenyan card gateways when processing direct digital payments.
  • Communication Platforms: Meta / WhatsApp, used to receive and facilitate order messages initiated by you.

6. Data Security and Retention

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to safeguard your personal data from unauthorized access, loss, or misuse. Customer records associated with completed transactions are retained only for the period necessary to comply with Kenyan statutory accounting, tax, and consumer protection requirements, after which they are securely archived or deleted.

7. Your Statutory Rights

Under the Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019, you retain the following rights regarding your personal information:

  • The right to be informed of how your data is collected and used.
  • The right to access the personal data we hold about you.
  • The right to request correction or deletion of inaccurate, incomplete, or obsolete records.
  • The right to object to the processing of your personal information.
